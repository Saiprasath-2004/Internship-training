About Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. Known as the Missile Man of India, he played a major role in India's space and defense programs. He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and inspired millions of students with his vision for innovation, education, and national development.

Throughout his life, he believed that dreams and knowledge can transform society. His contribution to science and his dedication toward the youth of India made him one of the most loved leaders in the country.
